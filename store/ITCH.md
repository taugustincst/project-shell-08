# itch.io page — Cartographer's Apprentice

A draft for a free web demo on itch.io. The web build plays the free
apprenticeship (Europe and the Americas, up to the rank of Journeyman); the
whole world is the Google Play edition, so the itch page works as a free demo
that points to Play. Nothing here is published yet: the page is created by
hand, as below.

The zip is `cartographer-itch.zip`, built with
`python3 itch_pack.py <repo> cartographer-itch.zip` (the marketing tools),
with `CREDITS.md` added so the About screen's Credits link works. It holds
`index.html` at its root plus the files the page loads, and nothing from the
Android, test or store side. In a browser the unlock card says the whole world
opens in the Google Play edition.

## Upload

1. https://itch.io/game/new
2. **Kind of project:** HTML. Upload `cartographer-itch.zip` and tick
   **This file will be played in the browser**.
3. **Embed options:** *Embed in page*; viewport **360 × 640** (portrait); tick
   **Mobile friendly** (orientation: *Portrait*) and **Fullscreen button**.
   SharedArrayBuffer: off (not used).
4. **Pricing:** *No payments* (free web demo). Never *Paid* here while the
   Google Play edition is the paid one.
5. **Visibility:** *Draft* until the page below is filled in and checked,
   then *Public*.

**Description** (itch takes Markdown)

    **Not a quiz. A deduction game about the map of the world.**

    You apprentice to a master mapmaker. Clients bring fragments of the
    world: a torn chart of a coastline, a sign in a language they could not
    read, the length of a shadow at noon, the width between the rails, a coin,
    a flag they are fairly sure they remember. Take them to the atlas and find
    the one land they all fit.

    **Tap a clue to shade every land it allows,** and watch the map narrow to
    one. Then plot the expedition through a true neighbour, under budget.
    Every clue is true of the place, and every commission resolves to exactly
    one land.

    **This web version is the free apprenticeship:** Europe and the Americas,
    up to the rank of Journeyman.

    **The whole world is on Google Play:** Africa, Asia and Oceania (151 lands
    in all), all five ranks to Master Cartographer, and forty prestige
    commissions contested by three rival workshops.
    https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.taugustincst.cartographer

    No timers, no ads, no accounts. Returning clients bring back the places
    you have found before, until you know them cold.
