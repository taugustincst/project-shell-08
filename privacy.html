Privacy Policy — Cartographer's Apprentice

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Cartographer's Apprentice ("the Game") is published by AugustInnovations LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game handles.

Information we collect

The Game collects no data and shares no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking, and it does not sell or share any information.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (your career, your atlas and settings) in the app's local storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data removes it.

Purchases

Europe and the Americas, up to the rank of Journeyman, are free. The rest of the world is unlocked with one purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles that purchase and its payment, under Google's own privacy policy. The Game only asks Google Play whether the unlock is owned, remembers the answer on your device so it opens offline, and never sees your payment details.

Network access

The Game makes no network requests of its own. Everything it needs is included in the app, and it plays fully offline. Only the Google Play app on your phone talks to Google about the purchase.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
